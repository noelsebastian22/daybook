Daybook

Four directions on the carry-forward idea, each as a full-bleed maskable 512 tile with light, dark, one-colour and 16×16 tests, plus a horizontal lockup. All artwork sits inside the 111 to 401 safe square; palette is limited to the brand indigos, the two inks, and the reserved green.

01

Carry-forward

the unfinished line, in motion

Three task lines sit at rest; the fourth breaks the baseline, lifts, and arcs up past the stack still travelling. It is the only mark here that shows the unfinished item actually moving, which is the one behaviour Daybook has that a checkbox app doesn't.

Below 32×32 the four-line build closes up. Ship this two-line reduction as the favicon and the small notification icon: same gesture, heavier strokes, one less rung.

02

Tick as trajectory

completion with velocity behind it

The checkmark is rebuilt as a flight path: light on entry, weight loading through the vertex, then a long exit that thins as it accelerates away. Completion stays the subject but gains momentum, so it reads as done and moving on rather than done and stopped.

03

Day stack in perspective

depth from offset and value alone

Rounded planes recede on a fixed axis, one per day, with only the leading plane bright, so the depth is carried entirely by offset and value with no shading. It states persistence clearly but says nothing about momentum, and the recession geometry sits uncomfortably close to a generic duplicate icon.

04

Monogram D

a counter that sweeps forward

A straight slab stem against a bowl drawn as a tapered arc that thins as it comes round and stops short of closing, leaving the counter as a forward sweep with a gap where the day hasn't finished. It carries the letter and the idea in one shape and is the steadiest performer at small sizes.

Recommendation: 01, Carry-forward

It is the only one of the four whose subject is the behaviour rather than the artefact. The stack states "a day's list", the broken fourth line states "this one isn't done", and the arc states "it goes with you". One idea, three shapes, no explanation needed. It survives the one-colour test intact, which the day stack does not, and it owns a silhouette no other to-do app has, which the trajectory tick does not.

  • Hold 04 Monogram D as the secondary lockup for favicons, social avatars and anywhere the mark appears beside other logos. It is the same idea compressed into the letterform, so the two do not fight.
  • 02 is the strongest drawing in the set and the most legible at size, but a tick is the category's generic asset. Adopting it means competing on execution alone.
  • 03 is the one to drop. Offset planes without shading read as duplicate/copy in almost every icon set, and the metaphor is still the paper page the current icon is trying to escape.
  • Green #10b981 appears in direction 02 only, on the tick itself. Nothing else in the set is completion, so nothing else earns it. Note that emerald on indigo-700 lands near 3.1:1, which is fine at hero size and soft at 16px; the ink-50 tick is the safer favicon if 02 is chosen.
  • Wordmark is Manrope Medium, outlined, 0.028em tracking, cap height 70 against a 176 tile. No webfont is loaded at runtime.
